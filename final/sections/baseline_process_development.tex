\chapter{Baseline Process Development}
\label{ch:baseline-process}

This chapter will expand on the Semester 1 fabrication work, documenting all
process parameters needed to reproduce the depletion-mode GaAs/AlGaAs HEMT
array. The narrative should emphasise learning outcomes, yield, and open
issues that informed Semester 2 planning.

\section{Mesa Definition and Isolation}
Include lithography settings, etch chemistry, profilometry data, and lessons
on etch-rate discrepancies.

\section{Ohmic Contact Formation}
Detail the dual-layer resist scheme, metallisation stack, RTA profile, and
contact resistance characterisation.

\section{Gate Metallisation}
Summarise alignment strategy, Ti/Au deposition, lift-off cleaning, and quality
control images.

\section{Baseline Device Inventory}
Provide tables of geometries, wafer layout diagrams, and discussion of
process repeatability across the fifteen devices.
