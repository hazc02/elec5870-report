transistors, present the specific objectives of fabricating and benchmarking
recessed-gate GaAs/AlGaAs HEMTs, and highlight the dual-semester workflow
used in this project. Content migrated from the interim report will be
reframed to emphasise how the validated depletion-mode process underpins the
enhancement-mode experiments.
