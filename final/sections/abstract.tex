\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

\begin{abstract}
This report documents the complete ELEC5870M MEng project on fabricating
enhancement-mode GaAs/AlGaAs high-electron-mobility transistors via
controlled gate recession. It will first summarise the validated baseline
process and depletion-mode electrical benchmarks established during Semester
1 before detailing the Semester 2 process innovations that enabled
tightly-controlled recess depths. The final chapters will correlate recess depth
with threshold voltage, transconductance, and gate leakage to demonstrate the
tuning range achieved, and will reflect on the manufacturability of the
proposed flow.
\end{abstract}
