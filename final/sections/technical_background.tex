\chapter{Technical Background}
\label{ch:technical-background}

This chapter will consolidate the theoretical material that links heterostructure
design, carrier transport, and threshold voltage control. Existing text from the
interim report can be merged with new literature covering oxide removal,
alternative recess approaches, and reliability considerations.

\section{GaAs/AlGaAs Heterostructure Overview}
Outline the epitaxial stack, highlighting how the cap, donor, spacer, and
channel layers determine sheet charge density and gate control.

\section{Charge Control and Threshold Voltage Models}
Discuss parallel-plate approximations, quantum corrections, and the analytic
relationship between donor thickness and threshold voltage. This section sets
the theoretical expectations for the recess experiments.

\section{Gate Recess Strategies in Literature}
Compare wet versus dry etching, oxide mitigation strategies, and prior reports
of enhancement-mode GaAs HEMTs to justify the selected process window.

\section{Measurement Fundamentals}
Summarise the electrical metrics of interest (ID--VDS, ID--VG, gm, leakage)
and how they relate to the physics described above.
