\chapter*{Acknowledgements}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Acknowledgements}

I would first like to thank Rob Farr for his continual support, guidance, and
supervision in the cleanroom. It has been a pleasure to work with him this year
in such an excellent facility. As I begin my career at Arm in hardware design,
I wanted this project to give me a practical appreciation of the fabrication and
processing that sits beneath the systems being designed. The
experience of this project has done exactly that. It has changed my perspective on the link
between design and silicon, and has encouraged me to consider rotations closer
to the device and process side of semiconductor engineering during my graduate
programme. I am very grateful for an experience that has opened my eyes to this
part of the field.

\vspace{1mm}

I would also like to thank Dr Chris Wood for supervising the project and for
the helpful meetings that kept the work moving in the right direction. I am
very grateful to Professor Edmund Linfield for his insightful discussions on the
underlying device physics, which helped me understand the concepts needed to
realise working devices. Finally, I would like to thank the cleanroom staff who
supported my initial training in September, particularly Mark Rosamond, Rory
Gilroy, and Thomas Moorhouse.
